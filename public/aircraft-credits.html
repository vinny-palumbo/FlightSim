“Plane” (Cessna 172) by osmosikum, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

The source model is repackaged as glTF with its original 2K textures and geometry preserved. Aeronaut adjusts scale, orientation, and centering at runtime, and animates the propeller using the simulator's throttle. The source's presentation animation is not played. Exterior paint is recolored at runtime: blue becomes white, and white becomes golden orange-yellow.

The creator does not endorse this simulator. Practice scenery, fallback aircraft, flight simulation, and interface are by Aeronaut.
