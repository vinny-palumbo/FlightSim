`cessna-172.glb` is the original user-supplied Sketchfab GLB, including its embedded attribution and 2K textures. Aeronaut scales, centers, and reorients the aircraft at runtime and drives its propeller separately rather than playing the source presentation animation. No creator endorsement is implied.

For hosting, the GLB is unpacked into glTF, a geometry buffer, and its original images without reducing texture resolution or geometry detail. The original GLB is retained in the source assets directory.
